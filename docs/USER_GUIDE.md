# Questbook User Guide

Questbook is a free study planner that works like a game. It keeps your classes, schedule, assignments, study time, notes and test prep in one place, and you earn XP for staying on top of things.

**Open it at:** https://msherman16.github.io/questbook/

You don't need an account and there's nothing to download. It runs in your web browser.

---

## 1. First-time setup (about 3 minutes)

The first time you open Questbook, a setup wizard walks you through four steps:

1. **Welcome.** Choose **Set up my planner**. To look around first, choose **Explore with demo data** instead, which loads a sample semester. When you're ready to start for real, tap **Set up my planner** in the yellow banner at the top of the page.
2. **About you.** Enter a name or nickname and pick an avatar.
3. **Your classes.** Tap a quick pick (Math, English, Biology…) or type a class name. Then choose the days it meets and its start and end times, and tap **Add class**. Do this for each class. You can skip this step and add classes later.
4. **Study style.** Choose how long your focus blocks and breaks are, and set the theme and text size.

When you finish you earn your first **25 XP** and land on your dashboard.

> Want to redo setup? Go to **Help & Setup → Run setup again**. Your data is kept.

## 2. The Getting Started checklist

Your dashboard shows six first tasks. Complete all six to earn a **+50 XP** bonus:

| Task | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Add your classes | Classes & Subjects → **Add class** |
| Add an assignment or key date | Assignments & Dates → **Add** |
| Finish a focus session | Study Timer → **Start** |
| Make a flashcard deck | Study Games → **New deck** |
| Add a note to your library | Notes Library → **Upload files** or **Write a note** |
| Check off your first task | Tap the circle next to any assignment |

## 3. Everyday use

### Plan
- **Classes & Subjects:** each class has a color and icon that appear everywhere in the app. Tap ✏️ on a class to change its meeting times.
- **Schedule:** your week at a glance. Anything due shows at the top of each day. Tap a class block to edit it.
- **Assignments & Dates:** add homework, quizzes, tests, projects, study blocks and **key dates** (exam week, trips, report cards). Switch between **List** and **Calendar** views.
- **Prep plan:** on any upcoming quiz or test, tap **Prep plan**. Questbook adds short review sessions on the days leading up to it, spaced further apart the earlier they are (7, 5, 3, 2 and 1 days before).

### Study
- **Study Timer:** pick a class and a technique, then press **Start**. When the block ends the time is logged automatically. If you have to stop early, use **Finish early & log time**. If you studied without the timer, use **Log time manually**.
- **Notes Library:** drag files in or tap **Upload files** (PDFs, photos of the whiteboard, slides, up to 50 MB each). **Write a note** has templates:
  - *Cornell notes:* cues, notes and a summary
  - *Feynman:* explain an idea simply to find the gaps in what you know
  - *Blurt sheet:* write everything you remember, then check your notes
  - *Chapter summary*
- **Study Games:** make a deck by pasting one card per line as `term :: meaning`. Then play:
  - **Flashcards:** cards you miss come back sooner (spaced repetition)
  - **Quiz Show:** 10 multiple-choice questions
  - **Match Rush:** pair terms with their meanings against the clock
  - **Speed Recall:** type as many terms as you can in 60 seconds
  - Choose **All decks (mixed)** to mix subjects together (called interleaving).
- **Study techniques:** eight research-backed methods, each with a button that opens the right tool.

### Level up
| Action | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Homework / Quiz / Test / Project | +20 / +30 / +50 / +60 |
| Finish 2+ days early | +10 bonus |
| Each focused minute | +2 |
| Each flashcard you get right | +2 |
| Study games | +10 to +60 |
| Add a note (up to 5 a day) | +10 |
| Daily quests (3 a day) | +20 to +30 |

Your **streak** grows each day you study, finish a task or play a game. Badges unlock as you go. See **Progress & Badges** for all of them.

## 4. Install it like an app

Installing adds a home-screen icon and lets Questbook open without Wi-Fi.

- **iPhone / iPad:** open the site in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the site in **Chrome**, tap **⋮**, then **Install app**.
- **Computer:** open the site in **Chrome** or **Edge** and click the install icon in the address bar. Some browsers also show an **Install Questbook** button on the Help & Setup page.

## 5. Your data & privacy

- Everything is stored **only in your browser on your device**. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking.
- **Back up regularly:** go to **Settings → Export backup (.json)**. Keep the file somewhere safe, such as email or cloud storage.
- **New phone or computer?** Export on the old device, then choose **Settings → Import backup** on the new one. Uploaded files aren't included in backups, so re-upload any you need.
- **Clearing your browser's site data deletes your planner.** Export first.
- **Shared or school computer?** Use **Settings → Erase everything** when you're done.

## 6. Accessibility

- **Settings → Text size:** Default, Large or Extra large.
- **Settings → Theme:** Auto, Light or Dark.
- **Settings → Reduce motion:** turns off confetti and animations. Questbook also follows your device's reduce-motion setting.
- Everything works with a keyboard. In games:
  - **Space** flips a flashcard, and **1** / **2** grade it
  - **1–4** answer Quiz Show questions

## 7. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| My data disappeared | You may be in a private/incognito window, on a different browser, or your browser data was cleared. Import your latest backup. |
| The timer didn't make a sound | Check **Settings → Timer sound** and your device volume. Browsers only play sound after you've tapped the page once. |
| No notification when the timer ends | Allow notifications for the site when your browser asks. |
| An update isn't showing | Close every Questbook tab or window and reopen it. Installed apps update on the next launch. |
| A file won't upload | Files must be under 50 MB, and your device may be low on storage. **Settings** shows how much space is used. |
